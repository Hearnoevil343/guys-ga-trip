# Knee pads (research only, not in the gear list)

Started 2026-09-23. Re-checking because the current pick (a flat foam gardening pad) isn't what you want — looking for something that actually protects on rock/gravel, isn't bulky, and doesn't cost much. Prices from Amazon listings, re-check before buying.

## Answer in one line
**Gorilla Grip Knee Pads (strap-on, gel + hard cap), ~$20-26** — straps to your leg instead of sitting as a mat, so it moves with you as you shift along the creek, and the hard cap plus gel/foam actually protects on rock instead of just cushioning dirt.

## Why not the current pick
The current BUY_LIST item (Generic Foam Gardening Knee Pad, $8.99) is a flat foam square you kneel ON, not one you wear. It works for one fixed gardening bed. Panning means kneeling, shifting a foot, kneeling again, over and over on uneven rock — a loose pad slides out from under you and you're back to bare rock. A strap-on pad solves that; it stays on your knee through every reposition.

## Recommendation
Gorilla Grip strap-on knee pads, ~$20-26 for a pair. Cheap enough, low-profile (not a bulky mat), and the hard cap is what actually stops you from feeling every rock — foam alone (even 1.5" thick) still telegraphs sharp gravel over time. WORKPRO is the fallback if Gorilla Grip is out of stock or pricier at buy time — same idea, slightly less tested.

## Budget/low-profile alternative
**Bodyprox Protective Knee Pads (Thick Sponge Anti-Slip Knee Sleeve)** — strap-on sleeve, ⅞" foam sponge, no hard outer cap, ~$10-13 (price not confirmed live, check before buying). Thinner and more flexible than Gorilla Grip — less bulky, easier to move in — but without the hard cap it telegraphs sharp gravel more, and reviews report the foam flattening out over time. Fine on soil/soft ground; the weaker of the two on actual creek rock.
