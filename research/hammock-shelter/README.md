# Hammock sleep shelter: options (research only, not in the gear list)

Started 2026-09-23. Prices/weights come from review sites (CleverHiker, OutdoorGearLab), NOT vendor pages. Re-check before buying. Underquilt/tarp figures are rough ranges.

## The "hack setup" that beats a tent
A hammock bug net + a hex/cat-cut tarp + an underquilt. Cold air steals heat from below (a hammock compresses your bag), so the underquilt is the part that makes it work. Skip it and you are cold below 65F even with a good bag. A sleeping pad in the hammock is the cheap fix, but slides around.

## Build your own (best sleep, most "survivor hack")
| Part | Pick | Price | Wt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hammock + net | Warbonnet Blackbird (top rated, integrated net, shelf) | $175 | 15.7 oz |
| Same, more comfort | Warbonnet Ridgerunner (flat lay, spreader bars) | $220 | 2 lb 9 oz |
| Same, budget | Onewind Tempest (integrated net) | $75 | 1 lb 3 oz |
| Same, modular | Dutchware Chameleon (removable net, spreader bar) | $152 | 1 lb 5.6 oz |
| Tarp | Hammock Gear / Dutchware / Onewind hex, 10-11 ft | ~$60-150 | 10-16 oz |
| Insulation | Underquilt (Hammock Gear, Warbonnet, Onewind) | ~$150-300 | 12-24 oz |
| Suspension | Straps + carabiner/whoopie slings | ~$20-40 | 4-8 oz |

Realistic totals: budget build ~$300, 3 lb; nice build ~$550-650, 3.5-4 lb.

## Top picks
1. **Easiest and very good:** Kammok Mantis UL kit, then add an underquilt.
2. **Best sleep for a trip this important:** Warbonnet Blackbird + tarp + underquilt.
3. **Value:** Onewind Tempest + a Onewind tarp/underquilt.

## Open questions for the Capitan
- Trip dates/night lows? Decides underquilt weight (or pad-only).
- Enough trees where you camp? A hammock needs two trees 12-15 ft apart. No trees = tent.
- Budget ceiling?
