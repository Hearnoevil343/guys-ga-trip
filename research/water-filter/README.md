# Water filter: bottle + bulk (research only, not in the gear list)

Started 2026-09-23. Prices/weights from Switchback Travel and other review sites, NOT vendor pages. Re-check before buying. I read "limetime bottle" as a LifeStraw-style filter bottle; correct me if you meant something else.

## Answer in one line
**Bottle on the fly:** LifeStraw Peak Squeeze 1L ($44) or Grayl GeoPress ($100, adds virus protection). **Bulk water:** Platypus GravityWorks 4L ($135).

Not recommended: plain LifeStraw straw (slow, awkward). All hollow-fiber filters (Sawyer, BeFree, Peak Squeeze) stop bacteria and protozoa, not viruses. In most US backcountry water that is the accepted risk. Only pay for viruses (Grayl) if sources are near livestock, camps or sewage.

## Recommended combo
- **Best:** LifeStraw Peak Squeeze 1L ($44) + Platypus GravityWorks 4L ($135) = about $180. A sink-quality bulk setup plus a bottle for sipping.
- **Cheaper:** Sawyer Squeeze ($46) alone. It works as the bottle filter and can be run gravity-style off a bag. Add a bigger bag for bulk.
- **Virus-safe:** swap the bottle for a Grayl GeoPress, or carry Katadyn Micropur tablets ($18) as a backup.

## Gold-panning note
Streams that carry silt clog hollow-fiber filters fast. Let water settle or prefilter through a bandana, and carry a backflush syringe.

## Open questions for the Capitan
- Solo or with others? Groups need the GravityWorks.
- Are sources near cattle or campgrounds? Decides if you need virus protection.
- Do you want a real bottle (Grayl) or a squeeze bottle?
