## Backcountry (decided 2026-09-21; source: research\backcountry-route-design.md, geometry: map\data\days.json)
Centerpiece: East Fork Coosa Creek (Union Co.). Gold record: Georgia Geological Survey Bulletin 19 (1909) pp. 237-239 and USGS MRDS Coosa Creek Placer Mine (past producer). Pan only on National Forest reaches, upstream of 34.80637, -83.95980. Nothing is ranger-confirmed — every creek stop is amber; calls in CALLS.md #1.
- **Long — 4 days (3 without the layover), Sat Oct 17 – Tue Oct 20.** Coosa Backcountry Trail from site P to West Fork Wolf Creek (3.53 mi, pan) → over Calf Stomp Gap to East Fork Coosa Creek camp (5.24 mi, pan) → optional layover (pan only) → out Bowers Road to Owltown Gap (2.29 mi), drive to Vogel. Truck 2 staged at Owltown Gap Fri evening.
- **Short — 2 days / 1 night, Mon Oct 19 – Tue Oct 20.** Owltown Gap → Bowers Road → camp (2.29 mi, pan), out via Calf Stomp Gap and the trail to a truck on FS 107 (5.24 mi). Fallback if FS 107 is gated: walk back out Bowers Road. Oct 15-18 unchanged (Cooper Creek, GA-348 loop).
