# Backcountry route design — East Fork Coosa Creek (2026-09-21)

Designed by the route-design session. Every claim below was checked first-hand by that
session (not only by an agent) unless marked UNCONFIRMED. The map must show anything
UNCONFIRMED as amber. Nothing here is green: no ranger has confirmed any specific creek.

## Why this drainage

**Gold record (primary source, read first-hand):** Georgia Geological Survey Bulletin 19,
S.P. Jones, 1909, "Second Report on the Gold Deposits of Georgia", pp. 237-239. Full text
saved at `research/bulletin19-fulltext.txt`; record
https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_ggpd_s-ga-bm500-pg4-bb1-bno-p19

> "Placer deposits along Coosa Creek have been mined at different intervals for a long
> period of years. ... especially near its head, the stream course is in V-shaped defiles
> and the auriferous deposits are confined to very narrow alluvial strips or to the bed of
> the creek only. ... the deposits have been mined from near the headwaters of the stream
> high up on a mountain side, for a distance of several miles ... The entire output of the
> Coosa Creek placers has been variously estimated at from a half to a million
> pennyweights of gold. ... its purity is as great as .980."

> "The placers along Coosa Creek have yielded large amounts of gold and the mountain
> slopes of that region deserve careful prospecting."

Also: prospect tunnel on "lot 123, 10th district ... Close to the headwaters of the
creek", and "Work of a similar character has also been done on a hill above Owl Town Gap."

**USGS MRDS (fetched first-hand):** Coosa Creek Placer Mine, dep_id 10084699, Past
Producer, deposit size Medium, refs Bull. 4-A + Bull. 19 (location accuracy 10 km);
Coosa Creek Gold Mine, dep_id 10240673, plant at 34.80842,-83.9563 (accuracy 100 m),
ownership Private. https://mrdata.usgs.gov/mrds/show-mrds.php?dep_id=10084699 and
...dep_id=10240673

**Land lots (Georgia GIO statewide land-lot service, queried first-hand):** the creek
junction at 34.80137,-83.96678 is in lot 130, 10th district; 1 mi upstream is lot 131;
the FS/private boundary and the mine plant are in lot 124; Bulletin 19's lot 123 centroid
is 34.80811,-83.96506 — adjacent. So the public reach sits directly beside the lots the
bulletin names. (A local-history article naming lots 124/129/130 as Summerour mine lots
exists at sites.rootsweb.com/~gaunion/mm052704.htm but returned 403 — UNCONFIRMED,
search snippet only. Do not cite it as fact.)

**Ownership (USFS EDW BasicOwnership point queries, re-sampled first-hand every 250 m —
the survey agent's "all FS" claim was WRONG and is superseded by this):**
East Fork Coosa Creek: source 34.78995,-83.99150 down to 34.80637,-83.95980 = USDA
FOREST SERVICE (2.54 creek-miles). Below that NON-FS for 1.6 mi, a 0.5 mi FS piece
(34.81982,-83.96346 to 34.81950,-83.97147), then private to the mouth. West Fork Coosa
Creek and the mainstem are private. West Fork Wolf Creek: FS from source to
34.79131,-83.91724 (3.0 mi). **Pan only on the FS reaches. The downstream limit on the
East Fork is 34.80637,-83.95980 — everything below is private.**

**Pressure:** no modern panning write-up, forum thread, or guide entry found for the East
Fork (one search pass — absence of evidence, UNCONFIRMED). Last documented working ~1909-12.
Not a named recreation site. The access tracks may be open to vehicles (UNCONFIRMED) —
hunters/car campers possible.

**Geology / where to look:** gradient data from the survey: East Fork is steep at its
source (350-720 ft/mi), flattens around 34.798,-83.976, and re-steepens below the
Jones Branch (34.797713,-83.977594) and Roaring Fork (34.801680,-83.967784)
confluences. Flat-after-steep plus a confluence is the classic drop zone. Bulletin 19 says
upper-creek gold is in "the bed of the creek only" — work bedrock cracks and the inside of
bends, not banks (bank digging is not allowed anyway).

**Rejected alternative — Noontootla/Three Forks:** it does have a record (Bulletin 19
pp. 277-278; MRDS 10083733), but the named lots are downstream: lot 285/7th district
(Rantze Hill) centroid 34.745,-84.2226 is mixed/private; the FS reach of the creek
(Three Forks to 34.6956,-84.2149) is UPSTREAM of the mineralised ground, camping there is
gated on the unobtainable FS-58 order, and it is a 111-minute drive each way.

## Legality text to attach to every creek stop (all AMBER)

- **Panning — state AMBER.** "Recreational panning for gold in most stream beds is
  allowed. Special permission, permits, or fees are not required as long as significant
  stream disturbance does not occur and when only a small hand shovel or trowel and a pan
  are used. In-stream sluices and suction dredges are NOT allowed." — USFS
  Chattahoochee-Oconee FAQ, https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/about-area/faqs
  (quote verified first-hand 2026-09-21). "Most stream beds" is not creek-specific: no
  ranger has confirmed this creek. Call Blue Ridge RD 706-745-6928.
- **Camping — state AMBER.** Dispersed camping on general National Forest land. The
  Forest Supervisor's Orders page
  (https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/chattahoochee-oconee/about-area/forest-supervisors-orders,
  checked first-hand) lists no order naming Coosa Creek, Coosa Bald, Calf Stomp, Wolf
  Creek or Duncan Ridge. Coosa Bald National Scenic Area statute (16 USC 460ggg-1,
  read first-hand) withdraws the area from mineral *leasing* only; silent on camping and
  panning. Forest-wide order CO-08-03-00-26-01: max 8 people / 2 vehicles per site and a
  food-storage rule (agent-read from the signed PDF; not re-read first-hand — UNCONFIRMED
  wording). 14-day stay limit. Coosa Backcountry Trail overnight permit from Vogel:
  UNCONFIRMED (trail blogs only) — ask Vogel 706-745-2628.
- **WMA:** Georgia DNR WMA pages say "No fee to access land / License required to hunt or
  fish" (Blue Ridge WMA page verified first-hand; which WMA covers Coosa Creek is
  UNCONFIRMED). Firearms deer opener date UNCONFIRMED (DNR pages blocked) — wear orange.
- **Water:** the creek at every camp; treat it. **Bears:** no canister order applies
  (AT order CO-16-02 is Mar 1-Jun 1, Jarrard-Neel Gap only); hang food or use canisters.
- **Fires:** stoves only.
- **Access UNCONFIRMED (call-gated):** Bowers Road (FS 298) crosses private land between
  34.8064 and Owltown Gap — confirm it is open to the public and that a truck can be left
  at Owltown Gap. Whether Big Grassy Knob Road / FS 108 / FS 107 are gated is unknown.

## VARIANT "long" — 4 days (3-day = drop the layover), Sat Oct 17 – Tue Oct 20

All walking with full packs (pack_factor) except the layover (daypack).

- **Sat Oct 17 — Vogel → West Fork Wolf Creek.** Walk site P → trailhead → Coosa
  Backcountry Trail counter-clockwise → Burnett Gap → WOLF-X. ~3.4 mi. Pan West Fork Wolf
  Creek 3 h at/just below the crossing. Camp WOLF-X.
  Gold assessment for this stop: **no record for this creek.** Geological inference only —
  it lies between the Coosa Creek placers and Bulletin 19's "placer deposits near Crumley
  Creek", on the mapped trend of the Coosa Creek belt, steep with bedrock. Treat as a
  test pan. AMBER.
- **Sun Oct 18 — West Fork Wolf Creek → East Fork Coosa Creek.** WOLF-X → Locust Stake
  Gap → Calf Stomp Gap (big climb, ~+1,300 ft) → Big Grassy Knob Road down to JUNCTION →
  Duncan Ridge Conn upstream to CAMP-C. ~5 mi. Pan 1.5 h at ROARING FORK confluence on
  the way (pan leg with own lat/lng + at_mile), then 1.5 h at camp. Camp CAMP-C.
- **Mon Oct 19 — layover (OPTIONAL: drop this day for the 3-day version).** Daypack.
  Pan Jones Branch confluence reach 3 h; walk Duncan Ridge Conn upstream to where it
  leaves the creek (34.79824,-83.97825) and back; pan the upper "bed of the creek only"
  reach 2 h. Camp CAMP-C again.
- **Tue Oct 20 — out to Owltown Gap.** CAMP-C → JUNCTION → Bowers Road. Pan 1.5 h at
  BOUNDARY stop (last public water — do not go below 34.80637,-83.95980). Walk on to
  Owltown Gap truck. ~2.3 mi. Drive to Vogel (OSRM). Night at Vogel; Wed Oct 21 depart
  day unchanged.
- Shuttle: Fri evening, stage truck 2 at Owltown Gap (two trucks out, one back; 18 min
  each way). Put this as a note on the Sat day, and as a drive leg pair at the end of the
  existing Fri Oct 16 day.
- Oct 15 and 16 days: keep the existing days.json content unchanged.
