# Calls to make before the trip

Parked 2026-09-21. Nothing here blocks building the map or the plan, but the
first one decides whether the overnight happens where we've drawn it.

## 1. Blue Ridge Ranger District — 706-745-6928  ← most important
The order that governs the overnight. Ask by name:

> "Campfire, Camping, and Horse Restrictions at FS Road 58 and Chester &
> Noontootla Creeks"

- What is its **current text and boundary**? (Listed as effective 2013–2025,
  but the Forest Service extends these dates administratively without
  reissuing the PDF, and this one has no PDF attached at all — its status
  cannot be determined online.)
- Does it reach **AT backcountry camping at Hawk Mountain Shelter (~3.8 mi in)
  or Stover Creek Shelter (~1.5 mi in)**, or only the FS-58 road corridor?
  Best guess from the wording of sibling orders is road-corridor only, but
  that is an inference, not confirmation.
- **Are campfires allowed** at those shelters? Plan on stoves until told
  otherwise.
- Is FS-58 **gated seasonally**? Open in October?
- Panning legality on **Noontootla and Rock Creek** (general NF rule says hand
  pan + trowel is fine; confirm no local exception).
- Panning at **DeSoto Falls / Frogtown Creek** — the USFS page lists fishing,
  hiking, picnicking and camping but does NOT name gold panning. Our data
  claimed it did; that citation was wrong. Confirm it's allowed under the
  general rule.

## 2. Vogel State Park — 706-745-2628
- Confirm walk-in site P booking, Thu Oct 15 1 PM – Wed Oct 21 noon.
- Confirm 2 vehicles / 2 tents / 6 people at the site, and overflow parking.
- Current campsite and ParkPass rates.

## 3. Georgia DNR / Wildlife Resources
- Blue Ridge WMA: anything the group needs while camping/panning during
  firearms deer season (opens **Oct 17** — blaze orange).
- Noontootla and its tributaries are artificial-lures-only, 16" minimum,
  mandatory-release wild trout water. Does that carry any streambed
  restriction that affects panning?

## 4. Consolidated Gold Mine, Dahlonega — 706-864-8473
- Book the underground tour for 6, Fri Oct 16.
- Ask for the older guide specifically.
- Confirm flume panning is included.

## 5. Lumpkin County Parks & Rec — 706-864-3622
- Yahoola Creek Park: confirm panning is permitted. This is the strongest
  documented gold record of any spot on the itinerary, so it matters.

## 6. LDMA Loud Mine
- Guest/day access terms, if we want a pay-to-pan backup.

## Open questions that are ours, not a ranger's
- **Does the overnight stay at Three Forks?** Research says keep it, but be
  clear-eyed: Long, Stover and Chester Creeks have **no creek-specific gold
  record**. That night is about the hike and the camp. The documented gold is
  at Yahoola on Day 2.
- Cane Creek (Auraria) has the best creek-specific gold citation found —
  Upper/Lower Cane Creek Mines, Barlow Mine — but its public access runs
  through Camp Glisson, a private retreat centre. Worth a call only if we want
  to chase proven ground.
