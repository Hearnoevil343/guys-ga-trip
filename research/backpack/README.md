# Hiking backpack: options (research only, not in the gear list)

Started 2026-09-23. Prices/weights come from review sites (CleverHiker, OutdoorGearLab, AdventureAlan), NOT vendor pages. Re-check before buying. Goal: nice, comfortable, not a fortune.

## Hidden-gem pick
**Durston Kakwa 55** - $279, 30.3 oz. Reviewers call it near-perfect for the price: internal frame, big main bag, big pockets, tough Ultra 200X fabric, shoulder strap pockets. Beats Gossamer Gear Mariposa 60 ($315) on frame rigidity and price. Direct-from-Durston, so watch stock and shipping.

## Comfort-first (heavier, pricier)
- Gregory Paragon 60 / Maven 58 - $300, 3 lb 8 oz. Best overall, carry comfort 4.9/5.
- Osprey Exos 58 - $285, 2 lb 13 oz. Best ventilated (good for warm Georgia weather).

## Top picks
1. **Durston Kakwa 55** - best quality per dollar.
2. **REI Flash 55** - cheapest one that still carries comfortably; REI return policy is a safety net.
3. **REI Trailmade 60** - if the load is heavy (gold-panning gear, water) and weight matters less.

## Open questions for the Capitan
- Expected pack weight with water and food? Under ~30 lb: Kakwa or Flash. Over that: Trailmade or Paragon.
- Torso length / hip size? Fit beats every spec. Try on at REI if possible.
