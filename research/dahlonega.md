## 1. Consolidated Gold Mine (Dahlonega)

- **Address:** 185 Consolidated Gold Mine Road, Dahlonega, GA 30533
- **Phone:** (706) 864-8473
- **Website:** https://consolidatedgoldmine.com/
- **Hours:** Mon–Sun, 10:00am–5:00pm (open Mon–Fri and Sat–Sun same hours). Closed Easter, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day; closes early (1pm) Christmas Eve. Open all other days including mid-October. [consolidatedgoldmine.com/underground-adventures](https://consolidatedgoldmine.com/underground-adventures/)
- **Tour length:** ~40 minutes underground; tours depart every 20–35 minutes throughout the day, so no advance timed reservation is needed for small groups.
- **Price:** Adults (13+) **$24.95 + tax**; Children (3–12) **$15.95 + tax**; under 2 free. (Note: some older aggregator sites list ~$20/$15 — treat $24.95/$15.95 from the official site as current; UNVERIFIED whether this changed again by Oct 2026, worth a quick call.)
- **What's included:** Underground mine tour (guided, ~200 ft down/20 "stories") + gold panning instruction from "award-winning" panners + a complimentary starter gold sample to pan with.
- **Group booking:** No reservation required for groups under 25. Groups of 25+ should use the mine's "Group Tours" page/contact. For 6 people, no advance booking needed — just show up, though calling ahead is still smart for the "request a guide" reason below.
- **Underground conditions:** Constant ~60°F, slightly damp/wet, some mud. Closed-toe shoes recommended (no sandals). No strollers/walkers/wheelchairs on the tour (stairs involved). Bring a light layer — comfortable in short sleeves but 60°F will feel cool if you're not moving much.
- **What to wear for the tour specifically:** closed-toe shoes are the one hard requirement; prospector costumes (per your plan) are fine and thematically perfect — just avoid outfits with things that'll get muddy/ruined (skip good boots, wear expendable ones) and note stairs/slipperiness underground (per reviews).

## 2. Dahlonega Gold Rush Days 2026 & Helen Oktoberfest

- **Dahlonega Gold Rush Days 2026: Saturday Oct 17 – Sunday Oct 18, 2026** (72nd annual). Sat 9am–5:30pm-ish, Sun similar, per festival listings. Official site: https://goldrushdaysfestival.com/ — schedule page: https://goldrushdaysfestival.com/gold-rush-days-festival-schedule/
- **Crowd/parking impact:** This is Dahlonega's biggest annual event — estimates cited in local coverage put attendance in the hundreds of thousands over the weekend. **Downtown parking is effectively unavailable** during the festival; the town uses shuttle lots (Lumpkin County High School, UNG Dahlonega campus) with free shuttles to the square. Streets around the square close, with no parking after 6pm starting Friday Oct 16. The square itself becomes a dense street-fair (vendor booths, shoulder-to-shoulder crowds Saturday afternoon).
- **Does it affect Consolidated Gold Mine?** The mine is ~3 miles from the square (not directly on the festival footprint) but Hwy 19/60 traffic into town will be heavier and parking near downtown restaurants will be hard both days.
- **Recommendation for your Oct 15–21 window:** **Avoid Sat Oct 17 and Sun Oct 18 for your main Dahlonega/mine trip** unless you specifically want to experience the festival (bluegrass, crafts, food vendors, black-powder demonstrations — could be fun as a separate half-day if you don't mind crowds and go early/use shuttles). For the mine tour + calm town visit + lunch, **Tuesday Oct 20 or Wednesday Oct 21** look best (post-festival, weekday, minimal crowds, everything still open, fall color likely near peak). Monday Oct 19 is also a good fallback if you want a day of slack before departure. If you want to combine gold panning costumes + Gold Rush Days atmosphere, do a *separate* short visit Sat Oct 17 morning (arrive before 9am, park at a shuttle lot) and save the full mine tour for a quieter day.
- **Helen Oktoberfest:** Per Helen Chamber/ExploreGeorgia, 2026 Oktoberfest runs long-season (Sept 10–Nov 1, festhalle events Thu–Sun in Sept, then daily Oct 1–Nov 1) — UNVERIFIED exact daily hours in mid-October, but this means **every day of your trip (Oct 15–21) will have some Oktoberfest activity in Helen**, so expect steady weekend-level traffic and parking pressure in Helen especially Fri–Sun. Helen isn't required for the gold-mine plan, but if you also want to swing through Helen, aim for a weekday (Mon–Thu, Oct 19–21) rather than the Oct 17–18 weekend when it'll stack with Dahlonega's festival traffic on the same roads (Hwy 19/17).
- Sources: https://goldrushdaysfestival.com/, https://helenchamber.com/oktoberfest/, https://exploregeorgia.org/things-to-do/article/guide-to-helen-oktoberfest

## 4. Prospector attire (1830s–1850s Georgia/'49er look)

### Historically accurate elements (Georgia gold rush ~1829–1840s, overlapping California '49er look)
- **Hat:** soft-brimmed slouch hat (felt), or a flat cap; broad-brim "boss of the plains" style hats are more late-1800s/Western but read fine for the theme.
- **Shirt:** plain long-sleeve button-up (cotton or flannel/wool), loose fit, often collarless or with a simple collar.
- **Vest:** cotton, wool, or leather vest over the shirt — very common period layering piece, adds authenticity cheaply.
- **Suspenders (braces):** period-correct and practical — trousers of the era were cut high-waisted without belt loops.
- **Trousers:** canvas, denim (early Levi's-style riveted denim actually dates to 1873, so true 1830s-40s Georgia wouldn't have had blue jeans — but for a fun theme trip nobody will care), wool, or duck canvas work pants.
- **Boots:** leather lace-up work boots or riding boots; practically, anything closed-toe and sturdy works and is required for the mine tour anyway.
- **Bandana:** neckerchief, tied loosely at the neck — cheap, useful for actual sweat/dust on a real panning day too.
- **Props:** gold pan, pickaxe, small shovel, canvas sack/pouch for "finds," an oil lantern replica (battery LED versions are safer and available cheaply), a canteen.

### Budget sources & approximate costs (per person)
- **Thrift stores (Goodwill/local):** flannel/chambray shirt $5–10, vest $5–8, work boots $10–20, trousers $5–10 — a full base outfit for **$25–45** if you're not fussy about exact look. Best value, most "real" texture.
- **Walmart:** basic men's costume "Prospector Costume for Men" kit listed on walmart.com (hat + suspenders + bandana + facial hair piece) — budget kits typically run **$20–30**; plain flannel shirts, canvas pants, and suspenders sold separately are cheap too (~$10–20 each).
- **Amazon:** full prospector costume kits commonly $20–35 (hat, suspenders, bandana, mustache); "Gold Miner Costume" search turns up several options in that range; add a $10–15 gold pan prop for photos.
- **Tractor Supply:** genuinely useful for the *practical* half — real suspenders, canvas/duck work pants, flannel shirts, leather work gloves, felt or straw hats, all inexpensive ($10–25 each) and actually functional for real panning/cold weather, not flimsy costume fabric.
- **Western wear stores** (e.g., Boot Barn or similar, if one is near your route) — better-quality felt hats ($30–60), real leather boots ($60+), higher cost but durable and reusable.
- **Costume shops (Spirit Halloween, Party City, local costume shop):** full costume-grade kits $25–50, look more "costume-y" (shiny fabric, fake beards) — fine for photos, less practical for actual panning outdoors.
- **Dahlonega shops on the square:** several gift/gold-themed shops sell novelty gold pans, bandanas, and "prospector" trinkets — convenient if you want to buy props same-day, but likely pricier than Walmart/Amazon for the same item; good for last-minute additions only.

### Suggested tiers
- **Budget outfit (~$30–50/person):** thrift-store flannel shirt + vest, suspenders, jeans or canvas pants you already own, closed-toe boots you own, a $5 bandana, cheap $10–15 gold pan prop from Amazon/Walmart.
- **Good outfit (~$70–120/person):** Tractor Supply canvas work pants + flannel shirt + suspenders + felt hat, real leather work boots, a proper metal gold pan (also useful for actual panning), bandana, optional leather vest.

### Practicality notes
- **Cotton/denim is fine for the town day and the mine tour** (60°F, dry, indoor-ish) but is genuinely unsafe for a cold October night at Vogel — cotton kills insulating ability when damp. Pack real synthetic/wool base layers, a fleece or puffy jacket, and rain gear for camp; save the costume pieces for the Dahlonega day trip only.
- The **metal gold pan** is the one prop worth buying "for real" (not a toy) — cheap steel/plastic gold pans ($10–20) work fine for actual panning too, so buy once, use for both costume photos and real panning back at camp/river.
- Suspenders, vest, and bandana are all comfortable and non-hindering if you do any real panning in costume; boots should be ones you don't mind getting wet/muddy — bring a separate dry pair for camp.

## 5. Logistics from Vogel State Park

**Vogel State Park address:** 405 Vogel State Park Road, Blairsville, GA 30512. https://gastateparks.org/Vogel

### Drive times (approximate, via GA-19/60 or US-19/129)
- **Vogel → Dahlonega:** ~25–30 minutes (~26 miles). Source: rome2rio/general routing.
- **Vogel → Blairsville:** ~10–15 minutes (few miles north on US-19/129) — closest town to Vogel.
- **Vogel → Helen:** ~35–45 minutes depending on route (via Hwy 180/Richard Russell Scenic Hwy or back through Cleveland) — UNVERIFIED exact minutes, budget 45 to be safe given mountain roads.
- **Vogel → Cleveland, GA:** ~30 minutes (roughly midway toward Helen on US-129/Hwy 75).

### Groceries, gas, ice, firewood
- **Vogel State Park camp store:** on-site, sells firewood, ice, pancake mix and basics — smallest/most convenient option, limited stock.
- **Sunshine Grocery:** ~2 miles north of the campground; produce, local jams/jellies, general goods — closer than Blairsville proper.
- **Ingles Markets #98:** 207B Hwy 515, Blairsville, GA — full grocery store, ~15 min from Vogel, best for a real supply run (produce, deli, salad bar). https://www.ingles-markets.com/storelocate/storeinfo.php?storenum=98
- **Walmart Supercenter, Blairsville** — also available for bulk supplies, per search results (exact address UNVERIFIED, standard Blairsville Walmart location).
- Gas stations are concentrated along US-19/129 in Blairsville — plan to fuel up there rather than at Neel Gap.

### Outfitters for last-minute gear
- **Mountain Crossings at Neel Gap** — literally adjacent to Vogel State Park (on the Appalachian Trail at Neel Gap), operating since 1983. Carries hiking/camp gear: tents, backpacks, sleeping pads/bags, boots, socks, cookware, trekking poles, dry bags, hiker food. **No gold-panning-specific gear confirmed** — treat as your go-to for cold-weather clothing, boots, and general camp gear, not gold pans. https://www.mountaincrossings.com/
- **Gold pans specifically:** best bought before the trip (Amazon/Walmart/Tractor Supply) or picked up at Crisson Gold Mine or Consolidated Gold Mine's gift shop in Dahlonega if you forget one — both mines sell pans on-site (reasonable to assume, standard for these operations — UNVERIFIED price).
- For general last-minute supplies (batteries, tarps, cheap tools), Blairsville's Walmart/Ingles corridor is the practical fallback since Neel Gap is a specialty backpacking shop, not a general store.

### Rainy-day backup activities
- **Dahlonega:** Dahlonega Gold Museum (indoor, covered above), Dahlonega Magic Theater ("A Knight of Magic," ~75 min comedy magic show), wine-tasting rooms on the square, Consolidated Gold Mine tour itself is mostly indoors/underground so it's a good rain-day pick.
- **Blairsville:** a 16-lane bowling alley with arcade/billiards/snack bar (name UNVERIFIED from snippet — confirm before relying on it), Misty Mountain Model Railroad (large O-scale layout), Crane Creek Vineyards for tasting.
- **Helen:** Alpine Fun Factory (indoor/covered family entertainment center with go-karts etc.).
Sources: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g34882-Activities-zft11295-Dahlonega_Georgia.html, https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g34775-Activities-zft11295-Blairsville_Georgia.html
